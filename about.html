About Us

A student-run film society screening a different film every week.

FilmSoc brings film lovers together for weekly screenings of everything from timeless classics to modern favourites. Whether you're a casual watcher or a devoted cinephile, there's a seat for you.

What we do

Every Tuesday evening we screen one film, chosen and voted for by our members. Screenings are followed by a relaxed chat about the film — no exam, no essay, just good company and better movies.

Get involved

Membership is open to everyone. Come along to a screening, join our mailing list, or get in touch to help pick next term's programme.
